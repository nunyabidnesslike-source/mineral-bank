Our Mandate

Built on the Bedrock of the Earth

Mineral Bank was established in MMXXVI with a singular conviction: that the enduring wealth of nations lies beneath the surface. We serve sovereign wealth funds, family offices, mining enterprises, and institutional partners who demand counterparties with the geological literacy to match their ambitions.

Beyond physical vaulting and provenance assaying, Mineral Bank facilitates direct institutional mineral acquisitions — connecting qualified sovereign and institutional counterparties with verified physical resource holdings backed by rigorous geological due diligence.

Our founding charter enshrines an institutional commitment to precision — in asset verification, custody infrastructure, and geological intelligence. Every vaulting framework we establish, every custodial protocol we execute, is governed by the same rigour that defines professional mineral science.
